## 3. Recurring templates

- **Same-planet aspects — how they're calculated:** transiting planet vs the same planet's natal (radix) position in the Nifty birth chart, matching his stated "birth chart method using radix and transit." Ephemeris check against the Nifty index launch chart (22 Apr 1996 → natal Venus ≈ 16° Gemini): Venus–Venus quadrature 23 Feb 26 = 90.3° (his label: 91°), trine 10–11 Sep 24 = 118–119°, sextile 24 Jul 24 = 59.2°, Mars–Mars 09 Jul 26 = 45.3°, Uranus–Uranus 26 Jun 26 = 119.0°. Formula: separation = min(|transit − natal| mod 360, 360 − that); aspect fires within a ~1–2° orb. *(Analyst verification, 15 Aug 2026.)*
- **Reversal-date template:** "Based on past Nifty data, this setup has shown high accuracy for reversals. Whatever trend continues till [date], we may see an opposite move starting next trading day." Used for the Mars–Saturn Quincunx (Jun 25), Venus–Saturn/Neptune Quadrature (Jul 25), and Uranus–Uranus Trine (Jun 26).
- **Subtract-2-trading-days rule:** aspect effective date = aspect date minus 2 trading days ("Venus Jupiter Semisextile — subtract 2 trading days on 9th July"); stated explicitly once, and may explain other date offsets in his calls.
- **Holiday rule:** when the aspect date falls on a market holiday, the previous trading day's close is used (14 Apr 26 → 13 Apr; 21 Jun 25 → 20 Jun; 27 Jun 26 → Fri 26 Jun).
- **Poll-then-answer pattern:** the aspect is announced as a question ("bullish or bearish?"), with his own view posted later — typically at 3 PM.
- **Intraday timing:** direction flips pinned to clock points ("bearish after 1 PM", "positive after 2 PM").

## 7. Backtest results (added 15 Aug 2026)

All 18 testable concepts were backtested against **Nifty (2007–2026)** and **Bank Nifty (2000–2024)**: every exact-aspect date was generated from the Swiss Ephemeris and forward returns measured from the first trading day on/after each event, signed per his stated direction. Baselines: Nifty +0.22% per 5 days (56% positive), BNF +0.41% (55%).

**Headline: no concept clears a fair statistical bar.** Two cells reached p<0.05 — Mars–Neptune conjunction bearish on Nifty (−2.45% avg 5d, n=9, p=0.023) and Venus–Uranus quincunx on BNF (+2.0%, n=49, p=0.008) — but 36 tests were run, so ~2 such hits are expected from pure chance. Directionally consistent leads worth watching: Mars–Neptune conjunction (bearish on both indices), Mars–Jupiter semisquare (+1.4–1.9% both, p≈0.1), Mars–natalMars semisquare (mildly bearish both). The flagship **Venus–natalVenus aspects showed no edge at all** (−0.1% to +0.9%, all p>0.37), the reversal-template concepts showed **no reversal edge** (reversal rates at or below the ~50% base), and Mercury–Mars conjunction went the **wrong way** (market rose on average after it, despite the Apr-24 story). The ~56% positive base rate is why standing bullish calls look right most of the time. Full tables and caveats: Excel *Backtest* sheet.

**Video strategy test (added 15 Aug 2026)** — his YouTube rule "Venus–Saturn Quadrature reversal" (90°, orb 0; reverse of prevailing trend; 150–300 pts in 1–2 days): all 7 cited dates are real quadratures (±0–3 days), and all 8 events in his demo window were shown (corrected 15 Aug 2026: the full transcript includes the 17 Jun 2022 example an earlier summary omitted — no event cherry-picking in this video). But a ≥150-pt counter-trend excursion within 2 days happens on **39% of all days** anyway; on exact event dates the rule scored 5/8 (62%, p≈0.24, n too small), and across all 40 events since 2007 the percent-normalized version hit 57% vs a 55% base rate, with identical median excursions (0.75% vs 0.76%). The rule performs at the noise floor.
