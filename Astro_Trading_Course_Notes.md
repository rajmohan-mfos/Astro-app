## 2. Foundation: The 12 Bhavas (Houses)

Counted from the **Lagnam (ascendant)** — house 1 is the Lagnam itself; Rasi and Lagnam can coincide. The Moon stays ~2.5 days in each sign.

| House | Significations (as taught) | Market polarity |
|-------|---------------------------|-----------------|
| 1 | Self, state of mind, body, the problems that come to you | — |
| 2 | Money, family, speech, eyes, food, early education, new family members | **Positive** |
| 3 | Siblings, courage, victory, short travel, communication, media/press, brokers | Mildly positive (Upachaya) |
| 4 | Mother, education, vehicles, house, land, property, cattle, crops, own business premises | Neutral / sideways |
| 5 | Children, love, ancestors, mind, speculation, **stock exchange, gambling**, cinema, politics, research | **Negative** |
| 6 | Disease, debt, enemies, hard physical labour, delayed marriage — but for the market: **temporary income, gain over competitors** | **Positive** (for gains) |
| 7 | Spouse, **business partner**; also the counterparty | Neutral / sideways |
| 8 | Longevity, losses, accidents, scandals, theft, litigation, suicide-level negativity — **the most negative house** | **Strongly negative** |
| 9 | Father, fortune, luck, long journeys, temples, higher wisdom; second spouse | Angle house (reversal) |
| 10 | Career, profession, public reputation, social standing | Positive (Upachaya) |
| 11 | **Profit, gains, fulfilment of desires, income without investment, sudden luck** — the most positive house | **Strongly positive** |
| 12 | Losses, expenditure, hospitals, secrets, foreign lands, hidden planning | **Negative** |

### The two key groupings (memorize)

- **Profit houses: 2, 6, 11** → positive / bullish signals.
- **Loss houses: 5, 8, 12** → negative / bearish signals. **8 is the worst** (heavy loss); 5 and 12 are "medium loss". 11 is "heavy profit"; 2 and 6 are "medium profit".
- **Upachaya (growth) houses: 1, 3, 6, 10, 11 → bullish.**
- **Apachaya: 5, 8, 12 → bearish.**
- **Sideways houses: 4, 7.**
- **Angle houses: 2, 9 → the market does the *opposite* of what the planet suggests (reversal).** *(Transcript uses "angle/cone" — when 2 or 9 appears, expect the half-day to invert the other half's direction.)*
- These groupings apply both to market prediction **and** to everyday life decisions (buying land, launching a product, etc.).

## 4. Prasanam (KP Horary) — "The Gateway"

Based on **KP — Krishnamurti Paddhati** (the instructor credits K.S. Krishnamurti with simplifying traditional astrology). Historically, court astrologers answered a king's yes/no questions the same way.

### The central discipline rule

> **Prasanam is the gate. No Prasanam permission → no trade, no graph-based entry, no exception.** It is compared to a boarding pass: without it you don't board the flight, however good the flight looks.

### How to cast a Prasanam (using the AstroSage app)

1. Open **AstroSage → "KP Murai" (KP method)** → new horoscope.
2. Enter a name (e.g., "Bank Nifty"), **current location and current time**.
3. In settings choose the **KP OLD method** (not new; no TST).
4. Formulate ONE precise question, e.g., *"If I buy a Bank Nifty call option now, will I get profit?"* — never a vague "will the market go up?", and never two questions at once.
5. Close your eyes, calm the mind (pray if you wish), and pick a **number between 1 and 249** from the subconscious (e.g., 88). Enter it as the horary number.
6. Read the resulting chart.

### Reading the answer

- **The Question = the Moon** — specifically the lord of the nakshatra the Moon occupies (Moon's star lord).
- **The Answer = that star lord's own star lord** (the next level down). *(In the worked example: question planet's star lord was Rahu; its house positions gave the answer.)*
- **Validation of the question:** the *question* planet must touch at least one of **2, 6, 11, 5, 8, 12**. If it touches none of these six, the question itself is invalid — "you asked from the upper mind, not the subconscious; it doesn't connect" → re-ask later (1–2 hours or another day).
- **The verdict** comes from the *answer* planet's houses:
  - Signals in **2, 6, 11 → profit** (11 = heavy profit; 2, 6 = medium). → permission granted.
  - Signals in **5, 8, 12 → loss** (8 = heavy loss; 5, 12 = medium). → no trade.
  - **Mixing rule:** even ONE loss-house signal poisons a profit answer — "one drop of poison in a tumbler of milk". If 5/8/12 appears alongside 2/6/11, treat it as spoiled: avoid.
  - **3 + 11** = profit but with waiting/delay.
  - **1, 4, 10 connection** = satisfaction with the profit (without it you may profit but feel unsatisfied).
- **Rahu/Ketu caution in Prasanam:** if Rahu or Ketu turns up as the Lagnam's sub-star in the Prasanam chart, the prediction can flip 100% opposite ("Rahu and Ketu entered by deceit — they can lie"). Avoid acting on such charts.

### The 3 rules of a valid question (from Long-Term Part 2)

1. **Right timing** — cast on a good day (own Moon not in 5/8/12; not over natal Rahu/Ketu; good Tithi/Hora).
2. **Precise question** — specify instrument, direction, target and horizon ("Will Reliance cross 4,000 within 2 years?"), not "will Reliance rise?".
3. **Valid connection** — question planet touching 2/6/11/5/8/12 as above.

### Scope

Prasanam works for **intraday entries, positional/long-term investing (up to ~5 years), individual stocks, forex, commodities, crypto, gap-up/gap-down calls, and even non-market life questions** (business decisions, travel, purchases). Worked example: *"Will Reliance (at ₹2,500) cross ₹4,000 in 2 years?"* — answer planets fell in 3, 4, 5, 12 with nothing in 2/6/11 → verdict: it will approach (3,500–3,995) but **not** cross 4,000 → don't invest for that target; re-ask with a 3,000/3,500 target instead.

## 5. Personal-Chart Filters (check BEFORE anything else)

Two checks on **your own birth chart**, every single day, before Prasanam or trading:

1. **Transit Moon in your 5, 8 or 12** (counted from your Rasi/Lagnam): **do not trade, do not even cast predictions.** The Moon sits ~2.5 days per sign, so bad windows are predictable in advance from a Panchangam or app — you can list your good/bad trading days for the whole month.
2. **Transit Moon passing over natal Rahu or Ketu** in your birth chart: **avoid trading** — days of outsized profit *or* outsized loss; especially dangerous when trading big quantity.

The instructor claims this filter is why long-term investors win while intraday traders lose: untimed entries. Following it still leaves roughly **10–20 tradeable days per month**.

## 6. Panchangam Elements: Tithi, Yogam, Karanam, Nakshatram

Four things to check daily in the Panchangam (printed almanac or app). Each contributes positive/negative weight; **never decide from one element alone — always combine.**

### Tithi (lunar day) — 5 families

| Family | Tithis | Market meaning |
|--------|--------|----------------|
| **Nanda** | Prathamai, Shashti, Ekadasi | Happiness — neutral/positive |
| **Bhadra** | Dvitiyai, Saptami, Dvadasi | Auspicious starts — market good |
| **Jaya** | Tritiyai, Ashtami, Trayodasi | **Victory — the good positive for the market** |
| **Rikta** | Chaturthi, Navami, Chaturdasi | **Destruction/obstacles — the key negative; big gap-downs (1,000–2,000 points) come on these** |
| **Purna** | Panchami, Dasami, Pournami/Amavasai | Fullness — neutral/positive |

Also: **Pournami (full moon) = positive** for the market; **Amavasai (new moon) = negative**.

### Yogam (27 yogas)

- Positive: **Ayushman, Saubhagyam, Shobhanam** etc.
- Negative: **Vyaghata ("disease" in transcripts — very dangerous/negative), Vaidhriti (obstacles & sorrow)** etc.
- Yoga carries roughly half-weight alongside Tithi ("half of Tithi's effect sits in Yoga").

### Karanam (half-Tithis)

- 11 karanams: **first 7 (movable) = positive; last 4 (fixed: Sakuni, Chatushpada, Naga, Kimstughna) = negative.**

### Nakshatram

- Judge by its **planetary lord** using the table in §3 (Ketu/Saturn stars negative, Mercury stars most positive, etc.).
- Special combo: **Saturn Hora + Uttaradam-type (Sun/Saturn) nakshatra ⇒ strong down day.**

**Scoring approach:** write down each element as +/−, total the day's bias, then overlay the X-Y graph. More negatives stacking up ⇒ expect red/crash-type days.

## 7. The Graph Method — Predicting Tomorrow's Intraday Chart

### Tools

- A **Panchangam** (printed, any language — positions are the same) or the **AstroSage / "Astro soft" app** (Play Store; Panchangam view shows Moon, planets, Hora, Lagnam). Panchangam positions are taken from **5:30 a.m.**; for "tomorrow's" graph, use the next morning's data.
- Market session split: **First half 9:15–12:00 (X)** · **Second half 12:00–15:30 (Y)**. (Commodity markets: split their session similarly into two halves.)

### Step 1 — find X and Y

- **X = the lord of the nakshatra the Moon occupies** ("Moon's star").
- **Y = the lord of the nakshatra that X occupies** ("its star's star").

### Step 2 — count houses FROM THE MOON, by degree

- Count which house X falls in and which house Y falls in, **starting from the Moon's position — but degree-to-degree, not sign-to-sign.**
- **Degree rule:** if the Moon sits at 29°, then one full house = from that 29° to the next sign's 29°. From this 29° to the next = house 1, next span = house 2, and so on (each span 30°). A planet 1–2° either side of a boundary can flip a 5→6 (bearish→bullish) or 6→5 call, so always calculate, never eyeball.

### Step 3 — read each half

Combine **(a) the house number** and **(b) the planet's nature**:

- House in 1, 3, 6, 10, 11 → that half is **bullish**.
- House in 5, 8, 12 → **bearish**.
- House 4 or 7 → **sideways**.
- House **2 or 9 → angle: that half runs OPPOSITE to the other half** (if first half was up, an angle second half turns down, and vice-versa). On angle days trade cautiously and preferably only after 12:00, once the first half has shown its hand.
- Then temper with the planet: e.g., bearish planet (Saturn) in a bullish house (10) = "up-sideways" — a shallow grind up, not a big rally. Sideways planet in a bearish house (Sun in 12) = "sideways-bearish" — every rise is a selling opportunity.

### Step 4 — the X1 / Y1 override rule

- After fixing X and Y, check whether the **same planet's nakshatra appears anywhere else** occupied by another planet — that occupant becomes **X1** (for X) / **Y1** (for Y).
- **X1 and Y1 are counted from the planet (not from the Moon).** *(X/Y from Moon; X1/Y1 from the respective planet.)*
- **Override:** when X1 exists, **ignore X** and use X1; when Y1 exists, ignore Y. If both exist, the day's prediction = X1 + Y1. If only one exists, pair it with the surviving X or Y. (Y2 can appear similarly one more level down in long-term work.)

### Step 5 — Day-Lord rule

| Day | Lord |
|-----|------|
| Monday | Moon |
| Tuesday | Mars |
| Wednesday | Mercury |
| Thursday | Jupiter |
| Friday | Venus |
| Sunday | Sun |

- **If the day's lord itself appears as X or Y → that half turns bullish** (e.g., Venus appearing in X/Y on a Friday preceded a heavy rally).
- **The rule applies ONLY to X/Y — never to X1/Y1.** If the day lord shows up in X1/Y1, the rule is void.

### Step 6 — draw the graph

Sketch first half then second half (up-slope / down-slope / flat / reversal), remembering real charts print candle-by-candle noise around the path — "2–3 candles down then one up". The drawing gives the day's **overview**, not every tick.

### Worked examples from class

- **Sun as both X and Y in house 12** → sideways-bearish full day: "every rise is a selling opportunity."
- **Jupiter (1st house, bullish) + Rahu (4th, sideways)** → first half bullish, second half sideways-up.
- **X1 = Jupiter in 9 (angle) + Y1 = Saturn in 10** → first half reverses the trend; second half a slow bearish-planet grind up. Trade only after 12:00.
- **7 Jan 2022 (Friday):** X = Jupiter in 12 (down), Y = Rahu in 3 (sideways-bullish) → first half down, second half up; overlaid Sun Hora (9:24–10:21) down then ~100-pt Nifty recovery after 10:21 — matched the actual chart except a shallow final dip.

## 8. Hora Timing — the "Golden Rules" (Class 8)

Each hour of the day is ruled by a planet (Hora). The Panchangam/app lists the day's Hora sequence. Effects are strongest for **~45 minutes** of the hour; **position yourself BEFORE the Hora begins** — enter at 11:40 for an 11:45 move and you're late.

| Hora | Effect |
|------|--------|
| **Sun Hora** | Market **down**, especially near the open on Fri/Sat-type days; small recovery after it ends (~30 pts Nifty / ~80+ pts Bank Nifty) |
| **Saturn Hora** | **Definitely down** — expect ≥30–50 pts Nifty, ~100 pts Bank Nifty; with a Sun/Saturn nakshatra, 100 pts Nifty / 300–400 pts Bank Nifty possible. Buy puts / sell futures *before* it starts |
| **Mars Hora** (esp. Tuesday) | Negative bias |
| **Mercury Hora** | Market **rises** (e.g., "up to half"; on Wednesdays 75–100% reliability as taught) |
| **Venus Hora** | **The unexpected** — reverses whatever was happening (an uptrend suddenly drops, and vice-versa). Don't hold through it; buy the put AFTER the reversal shows |
| **Jupiter Hora** | Positive / amplifies the genuine trend |

**Weekday golden rules** (one per trading day, Mon–Fri): e.g., Tuesday = watch Saturn Hora down-move; Wednesday = Mercury/Venus Hora up-move; Friday = Venus-day surprises + Mercury Hora same-direction move (check whether it lands ~2 p.m.). Each rule has a **minimum expected point move** — combine the day's rule with the X-Y graph for entry timing. If a Hora is one hour, expect the tradable thrust in about half of it; a full-session signal is trend-based.

### Nifty vs Bank Nifty rule

- If Nifty and Bank Nifty diverge (one up, one down), **trade Nifty** — "Nifty is the first economic trade"; Bank Nifty is only Nifty's banking subset. If both agree, trade either (Bank Nifty moves more points). If global cues diverge, close your eyes and go with Nifty.

## 10. Weekly & Monthly Prediction (Key planet: Sun)

1. Don't start periods at the 1st of the month — **start when the Sun changes nakshatra** (e.g., 7 Feb, when its star shifts to Mars's star).
2. **X = lord of the Sun's nakshatra; Y = that lord's star's lord;** apply the same X1/Y1 override.
3. **Count houses from the SUN** (not the Moon), with the same degree-to-degree method (e.g., from 24° to 24° = one house).
4. The prediction covers the span until the Sun's nakshatra next changes (≈13–15 days). **Split the span into two halves** — first half behaves per X, second per Y (e.g., 7–14 Feb down, 14–19 Feb recovering).
5. Chain successive spans (next star = Rahu, etc.) to build the monthly chart.
6. Stocks: the stocks of the span's planet follow the same path (Mars stocks down 7–14 Feb).
7. Always confirm with **Prasanam** before positioning. A paid software option exists that automates the degree work.

## 11. Long-Term / Yearly Prediction (Key planet: Jupiter)

1. Check Panchangam: **which nakshatra is Jupiter in, and how long does it stay?** (It can sit 3–6 months, longer when retrograde.) Confirm it hasn't changed for the recent months and won't change within your horizon.
2. **X = lord of Jupiter's nakshatra; Y = its star's lord; apply X1/Y1 (and Y2 if it appears).** Count houses **from Jupiter**, by degree.
3. Split the 3- or 6-month span among the resulting planets (e.g., 3 months = 1.5 months Venus + 1.5 months Moon when Y1 = Venus, Y2 = Moon).
4. Trade the **stocks of those planets** for those sub-periods.
5. **Worked examples (July 2021 chart):** Mars in 6 (Upachaya → bullish) ⇒ Asian Paints bought ~3,100 ran up ~500 points into October; Venus/Moon second phase ⇒ Titan +250 pts, Grasim +160, Indalco +450–550, with ITC/HUL flagged.
6. Personal filters still apply on the day you cast the prediction (own Moon not in 5/8/12; not over Rahu/Ketu) — and **Prasanam first, always** ("Will I profit if I invest in Asian Paints for 3 months?").

For any individual long-term bet, the **Prasanam target-question method** (§4, Reliance example) gives the go/no-go and the realistic price ceiling. Valid up to ~5-year horizons; usable on stocks, forex, crypto.

## 13. The Complete Trading Workflow (Checklist)

1. **Own chart:** transit Moon NOT in your 5/8/12 and NOT over natal Rahu/Ketu. Else: no trading today.
2. **Day quality:** Tithi family, Yogam, Karanam, Nakshatra lord — tally positives/negatives (Rikta tithi, Vyaghata/Vaidhriti yoga, last-4 karanam, Ketu/Saturn stars = red flags).
3. **Draw the graph:** X/Y from Moon (degree-counted) → check X1/Y1 override → apply house nature (Upachaya/Apachaya/sideways/angle) tempered by planet nature → apply Day-Lord rule (X/Y only).
4. **Overlay Hora timings** for entries/exits; position *before* the Hora starts; respect minimum-points expectations.
5. **Pick instruments:** Nifty vs Bank Nifty rule; day-planet stocks (no Rahu combos).
6. **Prasanam permission** for the specific entry ("If I buy X now, will I profit?"). No permission → stand aside, whatever the graph says.
7. **Execute with discipline; then stop.** Don't robotically cast charts at fixed times; don't re-ask greedily after a good win; don't take entries "technically" when astrology said no.

### Practice regimen prescribed

- **Back-test:** draw graphs for the past 10 days and compare with actual charts (instructor claims to have drawn 5,000+).
- Do workouts the instructor sends (dates → you submit graphs).
- Watch each lesson twice; practise on weekends when there's time to be unhurried.
- Expect ~70–90% correspondence at best *(his claim)*; "no one can give 100% — small candles will go wrong; follow the major trend."
- Discipline is the differentiator: "It can be done with technique or astrology — it cannot be done without discipline."
