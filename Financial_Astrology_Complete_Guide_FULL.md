## 1. The 12 Bhavas (Houses) & Core House Combinations

Counted from the **Lagnam (ascendant)** — house 1 is the Lagnam itself. The Moon stays ~2.5 days per sign. The houses govern human psychology, personal financial capacity, and broad market movements.

| Bhava | Primary significations (life areas) | Financial & market signification | Market polarity |
|-------|-------------------------------------|----------------------------------|-----------------|
| **1 (Lagna)** | Self, mind, body/head, state of life, the problems that come to you | Market open, overall index state, baseline sentiment | Upachaya (bullish) |
| **2** | Wealth, liquid money, family, speech, eyes, food, primary education, new family members | Cash flow, revenue, financial accumulation | **Positive** (also an *angle* house in graph work — see §6) |
| **3** | Siblings, courage, victory, short travel (~100 km), communication, press/media, brokers | Brokerage, trade execution, media releases, short swings | Upachaya (bullish) |
| **4** | Mother, education, vehicles, land, property, house, livestock, crops, own business premises | Real estate, auto sector, infrastructure base | Sideways |
| **5** | Children, romance, ancestors/grandparents, mind, fortune, cinema, politics, research, **stock exchange, gambling** | Speculation, equity trading | **Negative** (medium loss) |
| **6** | Debts, disease, enemies/authorities, hard physical labour, delayed marriage, temporary income | Competitive edge, banking service yields, debt markets, gain over opponents | **Positive** (medium profit) |
| **7** | Spouse, **business partners**, public relations, second child, the counterparty | B2B partnerships, public transactions, counter-parties | Sideways |
| **8** | Major losses, sudden obstacles, accidents, scandal, theft, litigation, bribery, mental distress — **the most negative house** | Market crashes, severe dips, capital erosion, margin calls | **Strongly negative** (heavy loss) |
| **9** | Father, fortune/luck, second marriage, higher wisdom/education, long flights, temples | Long-term trends, global cues, institutional flows | Angle house (reversal) |
| **10** | Profession/career, social status, public recognition, TV/media fame | Corporate earnings, government policy, market leadership | Upachaya (bullish) |
| **11** | **Net profit, fulfilment of desires, income without investment, quick recovery, sudden luck** | Net trading profits, target achievement, dividend gains | **Strongly positive** (heavy profit) |
| **12** | Expenses, losses, hospitals, secrets/hidden planning, foreign lands | Capital drain, hidden short-selling, institutional exit | **Negative** (medium loss) |

### Core predictive combinations (memorize)

- **Profit houses 2, 6, 11:** 2 = income/liquid cash flow · 6 = competitive/service gains · **11 = net profit & desire fulfilment (heavy profit)**. Signal favorable days for trading, business expansion, asset purchases.
- **Loss houses 5, 8, 12:** 5 = speculative risk · **8 = severe crash, heavy loss, mental anxiety (worst)** · 12 = capital erosion/hidden expenditure. Signal periods to strictly avoid trading or committing capital.
- These combinations apply to markets **and** everyday life decisions (buying land, launching a product, journeys, meetings).

### House classifications for graph work

- **Upachaya houses 1, 3, 6, 10, 11 → Bullish** (upward growth and strength).
- **Apachaya houses 5, 8, 12 → Bearish** (downward trend and loss).
- **Neutral houses 4, 7 → Sideways** (consolidation/rangebound).
- **Angle houses 2, 9 → Angle Market** (reversal / pivot turn / inversion — the half-day runs opposite to the other half; see §6).

## 3. Personal Birth-Chart Safety Rules & Filters

Check these on **your own birth chart, every day, before Prasanam or trading**:

1. **The 5/8/12 transiting-Moon rule.** Count houses from your birth Lagnam/Rasi to the transiting Moon. If the Moon is in your **5th, 8th or 12th** → **strictly do not trade or predict**. During these ~2.5-day transits, psychological lapses and execution errors cause losses regardless of market direction. Because the Moon's schedule is known in advance, you can mark your good/bad trading days for the whole month from a Panchangam or app.
2. **The transiting Moon over natal Rahu/Ketu rule.** If the transiting Moon passes directly over natal **Rahu or Ketu** → **strictly avoid trading**. Trader trap: these are exactly the days traders place oversized bets (1,000–10,000 quantity) expecting massive gains — the transit brings extreme whipsaws, outsized profit *or* heavy loss.
3. **Prasanam Rahu/Ketu sub-lord warning.** If Rahu or Ketu appears as the **Lagnam's sub-star (upa-nakshatram)** in a horary chart → **cancel the trade**. Rahu/Ketu "entered by deceit" and create deceptive, up-to-100%-inverted outcomes.

The instructor's claim: this timing filter is why long-term investors win while intraday traders lose. Following it still leaves roughly **10–20 tradeable days per month**.

## 4. Panchangam Elements & Market Sentiment Filters

Four elements to check daily (printed Panchangam or app) and overlay on the graph. Positions are taken from ~5:30 a.m.; for tomorrow's session use the next morning's data. **Never decide from one element alone — always combine**, and tally each as +/− to score the day's bias. Yogam carries roughly half-weight alongside Tithi ("half of Tithi's effect sits in Yoga").

### A. Tithi (lunar day) — 5 families

| Family | Tithis | Market impact |
|--------|--------|---------------|
| **Nanda** | 1, 6, 11 (Prathamai, Shashti, Ekadasi) | Favorable start, steady momentum |
| **Bhadra** | 2, 7, 12 (Dvitiyai, Saptami, Dvadasi) | Stable, good trading conditions |
| **Jaya** | 3, 8, 13 (Tritiyai, Ashtami, Trayodasi) | **The good positive for the market** — victory; strong expansion (can run to circuit-limit-type moves) |
| **Rikta** | 4, 9, 14 (Chaturthi, Navami, Chaturdasi) | **Destructive / high risk** — obstacles & sorrow; sharp gap-downs, 1,000–2,000-point drops, heavy crashes |
| **Purna** | 5, 10, 15/30 (Panchami, Dasami, Pournami/Amavasai) | Fulfilment, completion/consolidation |

- **Pournami (full moon) = strong positive/bullish.** **Amavasai (new moon) = strong negative/bearish.**

### B. Yogam (27 yogas)

- **Positive:** Shubha, Ayushman, Saubhagya, Shobhana → support bullish continuation and upward stability.
- **Negative:** Vaidhriti, Vyatipata/Vyaghata, Vishkumbha → heavy downside pressure ("red-blooded crash days").

### C. Karanam (half-Tithis)

- **First 7 (movable) karanams = positive/stable.**
- **Last 4 fixed karanams — Sakuni, Chatushpada, Naga, Kimstughna = negative/bearish.**

### D. Nakshatram

- Judge by planetary lord (table in §2): Mercury stars maximum positive; Ketu and Saturn stars negative; Mars stars 60/40 bearish; Rahu stars extreme.
- Special combo: **Saturn Hora + Sun/Saturn-family nakshatra (e.g., Uttaradam) ⇒ mega-crash-type down day** (see §7).

## 6. Intraday X/Y and X1/Y1 Graph Methodology

### Session structure & half-divisions

| Market | First half (X / X1) | Second half (Y / Y1) |
|--------|--------------------|--------------------|
| **Equities (NSE)** — 9:15 a.m.–3:30 p.m. | **9:15 a.m. – 12:00 p.m.** | **12:00 p.m. – 3:30 p.m.** |
| **Commodities (MCX)** | first ~4.5 hours | ~4.5 p.m.–11.5 p.m. span |
| **Forex / Crypto (24-hour)** | first 12 hours | second 12 hours |

### Step 1 — find X and Y

- **X (first-half factor) = the lord of the nakshatra the Moon occupies** ("Moon's star").
- **Y (second-half factor) = the lord of the nakshatra that X occupies** ("its star's star").

> ⚠️ The uploaded guide stated Y = "star lord of the Sun" — that matches one worked example (where X happened to be the Sun) but not the general rule stated in class ("Moon's star is X, and its star is Y"). See Appendix A.

### Step 2 — degree-based house counting (from the Moon)

- **Never count houses from 0° of a sign.** Measure 30° spans starting from the **Moon's exact degree**.
- Example (Moon at 29°): house 1 spans 29° of sign A → 29° of sign B; house 2 spans 29° B → 29° C, and so on.
- A planet 1–2° either side of a boundary can flip a call (5→6 turns bearish→bullish; 6→5 the reverse) — always calculate, never eyeball.

### Step 3 — the X1/Y1 sub-planet override

- After fixing X and Y, check whether **the same planet's nakshatra appears anywhere else occupied by another planet** — that occupant becomes **X1** (for X) / **Y1** (for Y). (One more level, Y2, can appear in long-term work.)
- **Override rule: when X1 exists, X is completely discarded; when Y1 exists, Y is discarded.** Evaluate each half strictly by the surviving factor (X1+Y1, X1+Y, or X+Y1 as the case may be).
- **X1/Y1 are counted from the planet itself, not from the Moon.** (X/Y from the Moon; X1/Y1 from the respective planet's position.)

### Step 4 — read each half: house number × planet nature

- House 1, 3, 6, 10, 11 → that half is **bullish**; 5, 8, 12 → **bearish**; 4, 7 → **sideways**; **2, 9 → angle (reversal)**.
- Temper by planet nature (conflict resolution):
  - *Bearish planet in bullish house* — e.g., X = Saturn in house 10: house gives upward force, Saturn restricts momentum → **"up-sideways" slanted grind up**, not a big rally.
  - *Sideways planet in bearish house* — e.g., Sun in 12: **sideways-bearish** — "every rise is a selling opportunity."
- **Angle days (2 or 9 active):** the half runs **opposite** to the other half. Avoid morning trades; watch the first half show its hand and **enter after 12:00 p.m.**

### Step 5 — Day-Lord rule (see §7)

### Step 6 — draw the graph

Sketch first half then second half (up-slope / down-slope / flat / reversal). Real charts print candle-by-candle noise around the path ("2–3 candles down then one up") — the drawing is the day's **overview**, not every tick. Draw it fresh daily.

### Worked examples from class

1. **5 May 2021:** X1 = Jupiter in house 1 (bullish), Y = Rahu in house 4 (sideways) → morning rally, afternoon consolidation (sideways-up).
2. **25 May 2021 — angle day:** X1 = Jupiter in house **9** (angle), Y1 = Saturn in house 10 → first half inverts the trend; second half a bearish-planet slow grind up. Enter only post-12:00.
3. **Sun as both X and Y in house 12** → sideways-bearish full day; every rise a selling opportunity.
4. **7 Jan 2022 (Friday):** X = Jupiter in house 12 (down), Y = Rahu in house 3 (sideways-bullish) → first half down, second half up. Overlay: Sun Hora 9:24–10:21 down, then ~100-pt Nifty recovery; afternoon leg mapped through ~2:15 p.m. (pad Hora boundaries by 5–10 minutes). Matched the actual chart except a shallow final dip — "follow the major trend; small candles will go wrong."

## 7. Day-Lord Rule & Hourly Hora Golden Rules

### Weekday ruling planets (Day Lords)

| Mon | Tue | Wed | Thu | Fri | Sat | Sun |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| Moon | Mars | Mercury | Jupiter | Venus | Saturn | Sun |

### Day-Lord application conditions

- **If the day's lord itself appears as X or Y → that half turns bullish.** (E.g., Friday with Venus in X/Y → heavy bullish rally.)
- **The rule applies ONLY to primary X/Y.** If X1/Y1 are present (override active), the Day-Lord rule is **inactive** — do not apply it.

### Hora (planetary hour) rules

Each hour of the session is ruled by a planet; the Panchangam/app lists the day's Hora sequence. Effects concentrate in a **~45-minute window** of the hour. **Position yourself BEFORE the Hora begins** (≈5 minutes early; entering at 11:40 for an 11:45 move is late). If a Hora signal is one hour, expect the tradable thrust in about half of it; a full-session signal is trend-based.

| Hora | Effect |
|------|--------|
| **Sun Hora** | Market **down**, especially near the open; small recovery after it ends (~30 pts Nifty / ~80+ pts Bank Nifty) |
| **Saturn Hora** | **Definitely down.** Buy puts / sell futures *before* it starts |
| **Mars Hora** | Negative bias |
| **Mercury Hora** | Market **rises** ("up to half") |
| **Venus Hora** | **The unexpected** — sharp inversion of whatever was happening (uptrend suddenly drops and vice-versa). Don't hold through it; buy the put AFTER the reversal shows (~12:30-type timing in the example) |
| **Jupiter Hora** | Positive / amplifies the genuine trend |

### Weekday golden rules with point targets

| Day | Alignment | Expected behavior | Point targets |
|-----|-----------|-------------------|---------------|
| **Tuesday (Mars day)** | Saturn Hora | Guaranteed drop | Nifty 40–50 pts · BN ~100 pts |
| **Tuesday** | Saturn Hora + Uttaradam-family nakshatra | **Mega crash / heavy sell-off** | Nifty 100+ pts · BN 300–400+ pts |
| **Wednesday (Mercury day)** | Venus Hora, Saturn normal | Bullish move (~75% probability) | Nifty 30–50 pts · BN 80–150 pts |
| **Wednesday** | Venus Hora, Saturn positive | Strong rally (~100% as taught) | Nifty 50+ pts · BN 150+ pts |
| **Thursday (Jupiter day)** | Jupiter positive | Strong bullish trend | High-conviction upside |
| **Friday (Venus day)** | Mercury Hora (check whether it lands ~2 p.m.) | High-probability rally in the same direction; Venus-day surprises possible | High-conviction upside |
| **Any day** | Sun Hora at/near open | Down first, then ~30-pt Nifty / 80-pt BN recovery after the Hora ends | Minimums as stated |

**General minimum expectations when graph + Hora align: ≥30 points Nifty, ≥60 points Bank Nifty** per signal.

### Index selection & divergence rule

- **Nifty is the primary macro benchmark** ("the first economic trade"); Bank Nifty is its banking subset.
- **Divergence** (one strong, one weak, or global cues conflicting) → **trade Nifty only**.
- **Concurrence** (both agree) → either works; Bank Nifty yields larger point moves.

## 8. Prasanam (KP Horary, 1–249): The Gatekeeper

Based on **KP — Krishnamurti Paddhati** (K.S. Krishnamurti's simplification of traditional horary astrology; historically how court astrologers answered a king's yes/no questions).

### The central discipline rule

> **Prasanam is the gate.** No Prasanam permission → no trade, no graph-based entry, no exception. It is a boarding pass: without it you don't board, however good the flight looks. The graph tells you *what* the day looks like; Prasanam tells you *whether you may act*.

### Casting a Prasanam (AstroSage)

1. Open **AstroSage → KP Murai (KP method)** → new horoscope.
2. Enter a name (e.g., "Bank Nifty"), **current location and current time**.
3. Settings: choose the **KP OLD method** (not new; TST not required).
4. Formulate ONE precise question — instrument + direction + target + horizon (e.g., *"If I buy a Bank Nifty call option now, will I get profit?"*). Never vague ("will the market go up?"), never two questions at once, and state clearly whether it is intraday or positional — an answer for one horizon must not be traded on the other.
5. Close your eyes, calm the mind (pray if you wish), and pick a **number between 1 and 249 from the subconscious** (e.g., 63 or 88). Enter it as the horary seed.
6. Read the chart.

### Reading the answer

- **The Question = the Moon** — specifically the **lord of the nakshatra the Moon occupies** (Moon's star lord).
- **The Answer = that star lord's own star lord** (the next level down; in the worked examples this was Rahu / Mercury).
- **Question validation:** the question factor **must connect to at least one of 2, 6, 11, 5, 8, 12**. If it touches none of the six, the query "doesn't connect to the universe — you asked from the upper mind, not the subconscious" → **discard without predicting**; re-ask after 1–2 hours or another day.
- **Verdict matrix (answer planet's houses):**

| Connection | Category | Outcome & action |
|------------|----------|------------------|
| **11** | **Heavy profit / target met** | Maximum gain, target exceeded — high-conviction go |
| **2, 6** | **Medium profit** | Moderate gain / partial target — favorable for smaller targets |
| **3 + 11** | Profit with delay | You'll profit but must wait (may dip first, recover late) |
| **5, 12** | **Medium loss / capped resistance** | Price stalls below target — avoid target-based buying |
| **8** | **Heavy loss / crash risk** | Severe drop — strictly avoid |
| **1, 4, 10 connection** | Satisfaction | Without it you may profit but feel unsatisfied with the size |

- **Mixing (poison) rule:** even ONE loss-house signal spoils a profit answer — "one drop of poison in a tumbler of milk." If 5/8/12 appears alongside 2/6/11 → treat as spoiled: avoid.
- **Rahu/Ketu warning:** Rahu or Ketu as the Lagnam sub-star can flip the outcome 100% → cancel (see §3).

### The 3 golden rules of a valid question

1. **Right timing** — cast on a good day (own Moon not in 5/8/12, not over natal Rahu/Ketu, decent Tithi/Hora).
2. **Focused query & genuine intent** — precise target price and timeframe.
3. **Valid connection** — question factor touching 2/6/11/5/8/12.

### Walkthrough: Reliance target-price query

- **Query:** "Will Reliance exceed ₹4,000 within 2 years?" (price ₹2,500; seed 63).
- **Moon check:** connects to houses 2 and 12 → valid query.
- **Answer planet:** connects to 3, 4, 5, 12 — contains 5/12, lacks 2/6/11.
- **Prediction:** Reliance will **not** cross ₹4,000 in 2 years; price caps near ₹3,500–3,800 (up to ~₹3,995).
- **Action:** don't buy for a 4,000 target; re-ask with a lower target ("cross ₹3,000 / ₹3,500?") and act only on a clean 2/6/11 answer.

### Scope & conduct

- Works for intraday entries, positional/long-term investing (**up to ~5-year horizons**), stocks, forex, commodities, crypto, gap-up/gap-down calls, and non-market life questions.
- Don't robotically cast at fixed times; cast when the mind is settled. Don't greedily re-ask after a big win — return later. Batch long-term stock questions over days (e.g., 10 stocks, 1–2 per sitting, 1–2 hours apart).

## 9. Weekly & Monthly Prediction (Key Planet: Sun)

1. Don't anchor periods to the 1st of the month — **start when the Sun changes nakshatra** (e.g., 7 Feb, when its star shifts into Mars's star).
2. **X = lord of the Sun's nakshatra; Y = that lord's star lord;** apply the same X1/Y1 override (Y1 counted from the planet).
3. **Count houses from the SUN** (not the Moon), same degree-to-degree method (e.g., Sun at 24° ⇒ houses span 24°→24°).
4. The prediction covers the span until the Sun's nakshatra next changes (≈13–15 days). **Split the span into halves** — first half behaves per X, second per Y (worked example: 7–14 Feb down per Mars in 12; 14–19 Feb recovering per Venus in 2 — angle/recovery).
5. Chain successive spans (next star Rahu, etc.) to build the monthly chart.
6. **Stocks:** the stocks of the span's planet follow the same path (Mars stocks down 7–14 Feb).
7. **Prasanam first, always**, before positioning. A paid software option automates the degree work (enter the change date; no manual degree calculation).

## 10. Long-Term / Yearly Prediction (Key Planet: Jupiter)

1. Check the Panchangam: **which nakshatra is Jupiter in, and how long does it stay?** (3–6 months at a time; longer when retrograde.) Confirm it hasn't changed recently and won't change within your horizon.
2. **X = lord of Jupiter's nakshatra; Y = its star lord; apply X1/Y1 (and Y2 if present).** Count houses **from Jupiter**, by degree.
3. **Split the 3- or 6-month span among the resulting planets** (e.g., 3 months = 1.5 months Venus + 1.5 months Moon when Y1 = Venus, Y2 = Moon).
4. Trade the **stocks of those planets** for those sub-periods.
5. **Worked examples (July 2021 chart):**
   - Mars in house 6 (Upachaya → bullish) ⇒ **Asian Paints** bought ~₹3,100 ran up ~500 points into October.
   - Venus/Moon second phase ⇒ **Titan** +250 pts, **Grasim** +160, **Hindalco** +450–550; **ITC, HUL** flagged; **Maruti, ITC** cited as earlier long-term calls still in profit.
6. Personal filters still apply on the day you cast (own Moon not in 5/8/12, not over Rahu/Ketu) — and **Prasanam first** ("Will I profit if I invest in Asian Paints for 3 months?").
7. For any single long-term bet, use the **Prasanam target-question method** (§8) for go/no-go and the realistic price ceiling.

## 11. Other Applications

- **Gap-up / gap-down:** ask Prasanam the prior evening ("Will the market gap up tomorrow?" / "Profit if I buy puts at open?").

- **Crypto:** Prasanam long-term questions (e.g., a coin's direction next year); never convert a positional answer into an intraday trade.
- **Daily life:** the 2/6/11 vs 5/8/12 test applies to land purchases, product launches, journeys, presentations, meetings. Illustration given: an IPL batsman's transit Moon in his 11th coincides with a big innings; in 8/12, a cheap dismissal.
