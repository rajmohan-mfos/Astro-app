- Bullish: Rahu (note: scenarios also treat **Jupiter** as bullish — e.g.

- Sideways: all others (Sun, Moon, Mercury, Venus, Ketu)

## 2. House classification (degree-counted from the anchor)
- Ubajaya 1, 3, 6, 10, 11: bullish
- Abajaya 5, 8, 12: bearish (8 = heavy loss — dominates any planet)
- Neutral 4, 7: sideways
- Angle 2, 9: angle market (reversal / volatile pivot)
